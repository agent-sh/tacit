Tacit keeps memory for coding agents in Markdown and git. Use a local store or share one across machines.

- **Every line says whose it is.** Agents were acting on notes that claimed your authority without your words: on the Mac's copy of the live store, 1,164 live facts say you ruled, said, decided or want something with no words of yours behind them (995 of them "owner ruling" wording). Now every line tacit delivers carries its source, read from fields code checked: your rules as `RULE` lines as before, your other verified words as "you said (date)", your statements as "you said once", and everything else as "agent note (date)". A fact that claims your authority without your words, by the same check new writes already get, is no longer pushed by any hook until it is fixed; search, the tree and `tacit_fact` still show it, marked "claims owner authority without evidence". The store is not changed.
- **Claims get fixed first, never with invented words.** The rotation checks those facts before any other and may reword one as an agent's note, which replaces it; code refuses a reword that still claims your authority or quotes words of yours the fact did not already quote. The rulings audit now also looks for your real quote in such a fact, and a quote found in your history makes it your ruling again.
- **A note that claims your authority is an agent's note, for project status too.** The curator read an agent's note that says "Owner decided on 2026-10-06 to delete ..." as your decision and made projects dead on it. Now it uses the same check as delivery: a note that says you ruled, said, decided or want something, with no words of yours behind it, is shown to the curator as an agent's note. It is evidence like any agent's note, but it never counts as your word on a project's status or successor, and it cannot override a project's dates. Before each assessment, code first looks up such a note's quoted words in your history (your sessions, your notes in the app, quotes already recorded); where they are yours, the note becomes your ruling (or holds your words), so your real words still win. No model is involved.
- **A successor needs your words to name both projects.** A project is linked to a successor only where the words cited for it name both the project (or another name of it) and the successor, so the link no longer depends on which sentence the curator cites. It is also refused when the same words retire both projects together (a plan that folds several tools into one new tool names no successor). Words that name none make the project dead with no successor.
- **The judge runs on Opus.** The conflict judge, and the curator's rulings pass that runs on its route, defaulted to Haiku; every deciding call now defaults to Opus (you said: "but we said opus"). Only the verifier still falls back to Sonnet, for a batch Opus has no capacity for.
- **Your words beat dates.** When the store holds your word on a project's status (it is obsolete, to be deleted, paused, done, or replaced by another project), that now wins over its commit dates. Before, notes in other folders reached the curator only when it asked about a folder a second time, and a folder its dates had settled was never asked again, so next-task, audit-project and sync-docs stayed active by their last commits although you had declared them dead. Now code shows each folder the notes that name it before the first question, your rulings, statements and decisions first. The curator copies your words exactly and code checks them against the note, the way it checks your statements. The newest words win over older ones. On a clean copy of the Mac's shadow store, next-task now comes out dead, replaced by tower-crane, and sync-docs dead with no successor, on the notes of 2026-10-06, and mem-retrofit dead on earlier words of yours, where v0.9.4 had them active or dormant by their dates. audit-project, prepare-delivery, gate-and-ship and ship now keep their dates (active, dormant, dormant, dormant): the only note that said they are to be deleted is an agent's "Owner decided ..." note, and your 2026-10-06 words are not in that copy (it holds no session transcripts; on the store they are only quoted in an agent's imported memory note, from a session on another machine). drift-detect, which the same plan names as sync-docs, and sglang came out by their dates in this run because the curator cited no words of yours for them; two earlier runs made drift-detect dead and sglang paused on the same notes.
- **Unless you changed your mind, which code decides.** Your words stand until you really work on the project again after the day you said them: a session of yours that worked in its checkout, or a commit of yours that changes more than maintenance files. A dependency bump, CI or workflow configuration, a version or changelog bump, and any commit a bot made (dependabot, renovate, GitHub Actions) are maintenance, and so is merging a bot's pull request; a security fix that only bumps a dependency is maintenance too. Code reads which files each commit changes, from git here or from GitHub, never the commit message. Work before your words never counts. When you did work on it since, the dates stand, the record cites your words and the work, and an optional review item says so; nothing waits on your answer.
- **Successors.** When your words say one project replaces another, the old one is recorded dead with a link to its successor (next-task: replaced by tower-crane). That is not a merge: the old project's notes stay as its history. The tree and search say what replaced it, and a session opened in the old project's checkout gets the successor's rules lightly at the start.
- **Names code can prove are added by code.** Other names of a repository's checkout and its current GitHub name become aliases by code, with the proof in the record, instead of depending on the curator to add them.

Upgrading: this is store format 3 and search index schema 14 (the index rebuilds by itself) (a record's successor, and the words a status rests on). Run `tacit format --set` once every machine runs v0.9.5; until then the curator writes the status and its reason only. The first assessment after the upgrade asks again about each project with a note of yours that names it, once.

Builds for Linux, macOS and Windows on amd64 and arm64 include the CLI, the browser app and a compatible scrump redactor. Git 2.29+ is required. [Setup and platform validation](https://github.com/agent-sh/tacit/blob/main/docs/portable.md).
