Tacit keeps memory for coding agents in Markdown and git. Use a local store or share one across machines.

This release fixes how tacit reads your turns and how it dates a status that rests on your words.

- **A side agent's note is not your words.** Claude Code delivers a note a side agent offers ("Here is a note offered by a side agent:", its body quoted line by line) as a turn you typed. The dreamer read it as yours, and so did the owner history that every quote check, statement and assessment reads. Now the note is an agent's text (`P:` in the dreamer's digest, not in your history), and the reply you typed below it stays yours. The reviewer shows the note as what you answered.
- **Your words after a harness reminder are kept.** A turn that opened with a `<system-reminder>` Claude Code put in front of your message was dropped whole. Now the reminder is the harness's and your words after it are yours. One of the 4 such turns on the rig was "we are banking our work and releasing boxes, no more memra work for now".
- **Codex sessions follow the same rules.** A Codex session that Relay moved from Claude Code holds Claude Code's task notifications, continuation summaries and frames as user messages, and the dreamer took them as yours. Now one rule decides what in a turn is yours, for Claude Code, Codex and pi, in the digest, your history and the reviewer. A Relay hand-off notice is the harness's. Your answers to a Codex agent's questions are read from their `answer` field. An answer that is one of the options the question offered is your choice among the model's words, so it is not counted as your words. A slash command's arguments are yours in the digest too, as they already were in your history.
- **A status on your words is dated by the day you said them, and a pause is dormant.** A decision that cited your words as `owner_quote` skipped the status checks. "we are stoping development of memra. bank what you have in one branch, take down all servers" made memra dead, by owner, dated by the rebuild's run day. Now:
  - The assessment's prompt says that stopping, banking, pausing or putting work off "for now" is dormant, and that dead needs words that end it (drop, kill, delete, replace) or the forge showing the repository deleted or archived. The model copies from your words the part that limits them in time (`owner_quote_temporal`) and, for dead, the part that ends the folder (`owner_quote_final`). Code refuses dead on words it flagged as limited in time, and dead with no such words and no forge evidence. Your words on a note (`owner_status`) get the same time flag.
  - The words are dated by your turn (the quote ledger's `said`). A dead, shipped or dormant status on them stands over the dates unless you really worked on the folder after that day. Then the dates stand, and an optional review line says so. Work after words that say dead does not revive a repository the forge has deleted or archived since.
  - A ruling's or a note's words shown for a folder carry your day too, not the day the note was filed.
  - `tacit assess --recheck-quoted` asks again about every record whose status rests on your words. `--folders a,b` bounds a run to those folders.
- **The wider search shows your newest turns.** When a folder left for review was asked again, the search showed one turn per session, the oldest. In the Tower Crane session that was a side agent's note shown as yours. The session's deciding words came later and could never be shown. Now it shows the newest turns, at most 2 per session, each with its day.
- **A session counts on its own day.** Code's status from dates took a session that filed a folder's note on the day the note was filed. A rebuild files months of sessions on the days it runs. On the rig's rebuild, memra's stop session, which ended on 2026-09-28, counted as activity on 2026-10-25. Now a session's day is never later than your last turn in it. A repository the forge has deleted is never active, as an archived one is not.

Measured on a copy of the rig's rebuild store (cf8fd634, 2026-10-27). The owner history was copied read-only from the rig on 2026-10-26.
- **Your history.** It had 19,604 turns and now has 19,239. Side agents' notes left it: 180 typed turns in the transcripts and 223 copies in Claude Code's prompt history. Codex frames and notices left it too. 4 turns after a reminder and 9 typed Codex answers came in; 10 answers that were offered options stayed out.
- **The dreamer's digest.** The digests of 58 of 121 owner transcripts change. 436 lines are no longer yours: 180 side agents' notes, 94 task notifications and 58 other frames in Codex sessions moved from Claude Code, 53 front-end notices, 22 interruption marks, 19 question-reply frames, 8 continuation summaries and 2 Relay hand-offs. 63 lines become yours: 21 replies below a note, 4 sets of words after a reminder, 9 Codex answers and 29 slash-command arguments.
- **Quotes, statements and rulings.** None of the 3,133 quote ledger lines loses its turn. No quote rests on a note's body. 15 lines that quote your reply below a note now match the reply alone. One quote counted twice changes: one ruling and one statement whose words a Relay copy of a Claude conversation held were counted as said in two sessions. They are now said in one, and their day moves from 2026-09-07 to 2026-09-06. The one-offs step makes that ruling a statement.
- **Records (Opus 5.5, `assess --recheck-quoted` on the 8 records that rest on your words, $0.24).** memra goes from dead to dormant, by owner, on your words of 2026-09-28. ship stays dead, now dated 2026-09-23. Five agent-sh plugin repositories stay dead, now dated by the notes of 2026-10-06 instead of the rebuild's 2026-10-25. audit-project does not change. On the Mac copy no checkout of memra exists, so code could not classify memra's commits after 2026-09-28. On the rig it can, and if they are real work the dates stand for memra.
- **Session days.** The code status of 4 of 115 records changes; 3 of them would move from active to dormant by their dates.
- **Delivery.** The 276-case replay gives this release's output byte for byte the same as 0.9.9's.

Upgrading: no index or store format change. The assessment's answer may carry `owner_quote_temporal`, `owner_quote_final` and an `owner_status` item's `temporal`; they are the model's answer only, never stored. Before the rebuild replaces a live store, run `tacit assess --recheck-quoted` on it (bounded with `--folders` to the records that rest on your words). The statements step, the one-offs step and the rulings audit read the new history on their next run.

Builds for Linux, macOS and Windows on amd64 and arm64 include the CLI, the browser app and a compatible scrump redactor. Git 2.29+ is required. [Setup and platform validation](https://github.com/agent-sh/tacit/blob/main/docs/portable.md).
