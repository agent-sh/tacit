Tacit keeps memory for coding agents in Markdown and git. Use a local store or share one across machines.

This release takes the owner's answer in the session they are in. An agent relays it with `tacit questions close --proof words`, on every question `tacit questions` lists but a name more than two files hold. Before, a name two files hold, a question the owner had touched and a change to their ruling took their answer only in tacit app (#111).

- **An agent relays the owner's own answer.** Code finds their words in their turns, as for every words proof: exact, not a question of theirs, not negated, in the latest turn that holds them. They are the owner's answer, and the agent only passes it on, when:
  - they said them on or after the day the question was asked;
  - they said them after the owner's own latest word on the question or its facts: any record of theirs (a mark, a reopen, a keep in another answer, a note, This is mine, Not mine) or any commit of theirs in tacit app on a file of its facts. Older words never undo a newer answer of theirs, and the same turn may answer two questions on one fact;
  - the item of their turn that holds the words is on the question's subject (below).
- **What a relayed answer may do.** It answers the questions no agent may close on its own proof: a name two files hold, a finding the owner marked, reopened or left a note on, and one whose fact they kept in another answer. It may change their ruling, on words on its subject said on a later day than the rule: than the last day they said its quote, or than the day they wrote or last changed it. On the rotation's `check?` it may answer `retire`, which only the owner's own answer gives. A question of theirs that went moot closes on their answer as `moot`, and only the record changes. While a relayed answer stands, agents' own marks and closures leave the facts it kept alone, and the verifier does not take findings on them. The owner's reopen ends that. Without the owner's words every guard stands as before. A reviewer question needs no relay: code reads its answer at the owner's prompt, as before.
- **A name two files hold takes the owner's answer**: `tacit questions close ID --proof words --words W --answer keep:<path>|both|neither`, with `--harness`, `--evidence` and `--reason` as for every closure. Neither is new for a name: it retires both files, each by its path, and the owner's page offers it too. An agent's answer on a name that the store no longer says (a file put back since) is open again, and the owner's answer takes it.
- **The subject is read from the item of the turn that holds the words.** An answer is often a short line after a dash under the question's own words, which the owner pasted. The line alone shares few words with anything; the words above it carry the subject.
  - The item is the sentences or lines that hold the words, plus the answer's question: the sentences before the answer on its line, or, for an answer that opens its line, the nearest line above it, from after that line's own answer when it has one. In a turn with no answer dash, the question is the questions right before the words on their line, or the line above when it ends in "?".
  - So the question's words count, and another item's never do.
  - Words that are a question's alone, words that run into the next question, and words in a turn that does not say where its items end (`q; - a; q; - a`) are no answer.
  - The item shares three content words of four letters or more with the question and its facts, by their stems (two when one is a specific identifier). At least one of them comes from the facts themselves: their names, descriptions, repository or machine. The words every question of a kind holds ("Which one stands?") and the folder words of their paths (user, projects, machines) name no subject.
  - Every words proof reads the same item for the rule on retiring a fact that holds the owner's words. With no item, that rule reads the words alone, as before.
- **The record says it was relayed.** A relayed answer is a line of `review/decisions.jsonl` like every closure, `by: agent`, with `relayed: true`, the owner's words (masked), where and on what day they said them, and the time (`words_at`). It endorses nothing and makes no ruling. The page shows it as the owner's: "Your answer, relayed by an agent", with their words, the day and the kind of session, and Change this answer undoes it. `tacit questions` marks the questions only the owner's own answer closes (`words_only` in its JSON) and the answers only it gives, such as `retire` on a `check?` (`owner_answers`). A name's answers read `keep:<path>`, `both`, `neither`.
- **A reopen keeps later changes.** Change this answer on a Neither, for a pair or a name, puts back what the answer retired. A fact retired again since, on another day, stays retired. A file the reopen puts nothing back in keeps its `updated` day.
- Known limits. Each one refuses the relay:
  - Code reads the owner's turns on the machine the relay runs on, and a relay runs only on a machine that writes as the curator. An answer typed on another machine is not found.
  - The words are at least three words and 18 characters. To relay a bare yes, quote it with the question's words before it, as their turn holds them.
  - Text tacit leaves out of a turn, a paste the prompt history recorded or agent text the owner copied in, is not in the item. A question pasted that way names no subject.
  - An answer typed without the question's words, such as a numbered list of replies under an agent's message, names no subject.
  - These shapes are misread: an answer line that ends in "?", an answer line without a dash among dashed ones, an answer that goes on to the next line, a dash that joins two clauses after a bracket or a full stop, a question without "?" in a turn with no answer dash, and the same words under two items.

Upgrading: no index, store format or protocol change; the server needs no deploy. Install it on every machine that writes as the curator: relays and the owner's page write there. An older client refuses a name two files hold in `tacit questions close` and keeps the guards it had on the owner's questions, but not the new one: its agents and its verifier do not leave alone the facts a relayed answer kept. It shows a relayed answer as an agent's closure and offers no Neither is true on a name. It cannot put back a name answered neither: on a relayed one it offers no Change this answer, and on the owner's own its Change this answer records the reopen and leaves both files retired. Change those answers only with this release.

Builds for Linux, macOS and Windows on amd64 and arm64 include the CLI, the browser app and a compatible scrump redactor. Git 2.29+ is required. [Setup and platform validation](https://github.com/agent-sh/tacit/blob/main/docs/portable.md).
