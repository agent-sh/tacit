Tacit keeps memory for coding agents in Markdown and git. Use a local store or share one across machines.

- **Agents' prompts are no longer your words.** The dreamer showed every user turn of a Claude Code or Codex session to its model as yours, including sessions no person typed in: `claude -p` lanes, runner workers, `codex exec` runs and Codex subagents. A Codex subagent reports your front end (`codex-tui`), so the owner-words check took it for yours too. Now one function decides, for the dreamer and for every place tacit looks up your words (the quote check, the quote ledger, the rulings audit, statements, the assessment's notes, the verifier's words), from what each transcript records: Claude Code's marks on each turn and how the session was started, Codex's `session_meta` (a subagent's `source`, `thread_source` and parent thread, an exec run), pi's headless timing and subagent marks, and Hermes's owner sessions as before. A turn that is not provably yours is not your words.
- **The dreamer still reads agents' sessions.** What an agent found out is still worth an agent's note, so those sessions are dreamed as before, but their prompts come as another agent's (`P:` lines), and the prompt says such a line is never yours. Code checks what comes back: a rule or a statement whose words are only in an agent's prompt, or from a window with no turn of yours, is filed as an agent's note that says whose the words were. A headless pi run is now dreamed the same way; before, its prompt hid it from the dreamer unless a tool failed.
- **Your own sessions, turn by turn.** Inside a session of yours, a scheduled task's prompt, a background task's or a teammate's message, a plugin's turn, a subagent's sidechain and a turn a `claude -p --resume` sent are not yours either, and neither is a suggestion you accepted (the model wrote it). A prompt in your history is left out when its session's transcript is an agent's, or when it was typed in a directory only agents use. Your typed turns, queued ones and the desktop app's are yours as before.
- **Measured on the workstation (read-only, 2026-10-24).** Of 110 Codex sessions, 38 subagents that named `codex-tui` moved from yours to an agent's. Your words: 17,994 turns in 756 sessions before, 17,911 in 733 now (49 accepted suggestions, 25 history prompts typed in temporary, e2e or background-job directories, 9 Codex subagent turns, 6 desktop SDK turns). The dreamer, outside tacit's own runs: Claude Code user lines shown as yours go from 5,392 to 2,102 (2,685 in runner and scratch sessions, 511 in `claude -p` lanes, 94 inside sessions of yours), Codex from 992 to 937 (24 exec, 20 runner, 11 subagent). None of the 377 quote-ledger entries, and none of the 321 rulings' quotes or 42 recorded words of yours in the shared store (a copy of 2026-10-24), rests on a turn that is now an agent's: the dreamer's mislabelled turns had not made it past the curator's check. On the Mac the corpus is unchanged.

Upgrading: nothing to run. The store format (3) and the search index schema (14) are unchanged. Notes already dreamed keep what they say; the curator's check against your turns, which already left out agents' sessions other than Codex subagents, now leaves those out too.

Builds for Linux, macOS and Windows on amd64 and arm64 include the CLI, the browser app and a compatible scrump redactor. Git 2.29+ is required. [Setup and platform validation](https://github.com/agent-sh/tacit/blob/main/docs/portable.md).
